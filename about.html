About DormSphere

Built to Simplify Hostel Life

We're on a mission to replace messy registers and scattered spreadsheets with one clean, reliable system — so wardens can focus on students, not paperwork.

Our Story

DormSphere started as a simple observation: hostels everywhere were still being run on paper registers, WhatsApp groups, and Excel sheets held together by habit. Fee collection was tracked by hand, room allotments lived in someone's notebook, and complaints got lost in the shuffle.

We built DormSphere to bring all of that into one place — a fast, digital system that hostel administrators can actually enjoy using, and one that gives students a transparent view of their own records, dues, and requests.
